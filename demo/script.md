# Demo video script: Learn with AI (about 3 minutes)

Read the **VOICE** lines in a calm, warm voice, a little slower than normal talk. Record in a quiet room, phone held a hand-span away. Leave a two-second pause between sections; the pictures are added to fit your voice afterwards.

The **SCREEN** lines are what appears while you speak. Each section opens on a title card with its heading.

The story is why the world needs GrowthGoal. It is told only through what GrowthGoal gives people: it compares itself with no one and finds fault with no one. Teachers, parents and coaches appear as the people it works beside.

---

## 1. Everyone is learning something (0:00 – 0:22)

**SCREEN:** Four quick real-life moments: a child's hand holding a pencil, a grandmother looking at a payment app, a shopkeeper at a laptop, a student practising in front of a mirror. Then the GrowthGoal logo and "Grow a little every day".

**VOICE:**
Right now, someone is learning to write their first letters.
Someone is learning to pay safely on their phone.
Someone is learning the spreadsheet that runs their family's shop.
Everyone is learning something. GrowthGoal exists so that every one of them can grow a little every day.

## 2. Why it matters (0:22 – 0:40)

**SCREEN:** A phone opening growthgoal.in/learn; the home page.

**VOICE:**
We learn best when the lesson is about our own life: our examples, our language, our pace, and a clear sign that we are getting better.
Learn with AI, built on Meta's models, gives that to anyone, for any skill, at any age, for the price of a book.

## 3. What it is (0:40 – 1:00)

**SCREEN:** Type "public" in the search box, then tap the Seniors chip and scroll the shelves. Then the "Ask for any skill" card.

**VOICE:**
Three hundred skills, for every age. Name a skill that isn't here, and the AI builds it too.
For each one, the learner gets a workbook written just for them, help whenever they are stuck, a check of their finished work, and a skill badge.

## 4. A child learns handwriting (1:00 – 1:22)

**SCREEN:** A parent filling in the short form for a six-year-old and choosing Hindi. The workbook appears: the cover with a dinosaur and a cricket bat, then dotted letters to trace. A child's hand tracing. The parent scans the QR and uploads a photo. The result shows the letter boxes and the feedback.

**VOICE:**
Aarav is six. His mother answers a few questions: he loves dinosaurs and cricket, and his letters float above the line.
So his workbook is full of dinosaurs and cricket.
When he finishes a page, she scans the QR code and sends a photo. Meta's SAM model finds every letter, so the feedback is exact: "your second B floats above the line. Start it on the line."

## 5. An adult learns Excel (1:22 – 1:45)

**SCREEN:** Priya's form. After her answers, the AI asks two follow-up questions about her shop. Her workbook opens with her own shop's sales. She opens "Stuck?" on a task and reads the explanation. She uploads her spreadsheet; the result says D4 was typed, not a formula.

**VOICE:**
Priya keeps the sales sheet for her family's clothing shop. First, the AI asks what the shop sells and how many bills she makes a day, so every task uses her own shop.
Stuck? It explains a new way, as often as she needs, and leaves the doing to her.
When she uploads her file, the checker reads every cell, and spots the one total she typed by hand.

## 6. A student learns to speak (1:45 – 1:57)

**SCREEN:** Rohit taps Record on a speaking task. The result shows words a minute, filler words, and the feedback.

**VOICE:**
Rohit is getting ready for interviews. He records his answer. Meta's voice model hears it, and the checker looks at what he said, his pace, and his filler words.

## 7. Proof of growth (1:57 – 2:18)

**SCREEN:** A badge page: "Excel Basics, earned by Priya", with "+92 skill points". The leaderboard, with Priya's row highlighted. Back on the skill page, Advanced now shows as unlocked. Then a "fix-up workbook" opening.

**VOICE:**
Pass, and the badge says exactly what you can now do, and adds skill points to the leaderboard.
Each badge opens the next level. The AI reads the work it checked and starts exactly where you need it, all the way to mastery.
Not there yet? A free fix-up workbook for just those parts.

## 8. Built with care, on Meta (2:18 – 2:37)

**SCREEN:** A simple diagram: Muse Spark, SAM 3.1, Muse Voice Transcribe, Muse Image, each with what it does.

**VOICE:**
Muse Spark asks the questions, writes every workbook, teaches, and marks the work. SAM measures handwriting, Muse Voice Transcribe hears speaking, and Muse Image draws every cover.
Our learners include children, so we never use the mode where Meta can train on their work, and the photos they hand in are never kept.

## 9. Why the world needs GrowthGoal (2:37 – 2:55)

**SCREEN:** Three badges side by side: Aarav's, Kamla's UPI safety badge, Priya's. A parent beside a child with a printed workbook. Then growthgoal.in/learn, then the GrowthGoal logo with "Grow a little every day".

**VOICE:**
Every badge is a small, real piece of growth: a child who writes on the line, a grandmother who pays safely, a shop that trusts its numbers.
GrowthGoal works beside everyone who helps us grow: parents, teachers and coaches.
It's live today at growthgoal.in slash learn.
GrowthGoal. Grow a little every day.
