# Demo video script: Learn with AI (about 2 minutes 45 seconds)

Read the **VOICE** lines in a calm, warm voice, a little slower than normal talk. Record in a quiet room, phone held a hand-span away. Leave a two-second pause between sections; the pictures are added to fit your voice afterwards.

The **SCREEN** lines are what appears while you speak.

---

## 1. The problem (0:00 – 0:18)

**SCREEN:** A tired teacher at a whiteboard, then a phone opening growthgoal.in/learn.

**VOICE:**
In India, a private tutor can cost more than a thousand rupees an hour.
And even the best teacher gets tired of explaining the same thing for the fortieth time.
An AI teacher doesn't.

## 2. What it is (0:18 – 0:40)

**SCREEN:** The Learn with AI home page. Type "public" in the search box, then tap the Seniors chip and scroll the shelves. Then the "Ask for any skill" card.

**VOICE:**
This is Learn with AI, by GrowthGoal.
Three hundred skills, for every age, from three to eighty: handwriting, Excel, public speaking, parenting, UPI safety.
And if your skill isn't on the list, name it, and the AI builds its workbook too.
For each one, the AI writes a workbook just for you, helps whenever you are stuck, checks your finished work, and gives you a skill badge.

## 3. A child learns handwriting (0:40 – 1:05)

**SCREEN:** A parent filling in the short form for a six-year-old and choosing Hindi. The workbook appears: the cover with a dinosaur and a cricket bat, then dotted letters to trace. A child's hand tracing. The parent scans the QR and uploads a photo. The result shows the letter boxes and the feedback.

**VOICE:**
Aarav is six. His mother answers a few questions about him: what he loves, and that his letters float above the line.
In a minute, Aarav has his own workbook: dinosaurs and cricket, because that is what he loves.
When he finishes a page, his mother scans the QR code and sends a photo.
Meta's SAM model finds every single letter. So the feedback is exact: "your second B floats above the line. Start it on the line."

## 4. An adult learns Excel (1:05 – 1:32)

**SCREEN:** Priya's form. After her answers, the AI asks two follow-up questions about her shop. Her workbook opens with her own shop's sales. She opens "Stuck?" on a task, then the "Open in ChatGPT" and "Open in Claude" buttons. She uploads her spreadsheet; the result says D4 was typed, not a formula.

**VOICE:**
Priya runs the sales sheet at her family's clothing shop.
Before it writes a word, the AI asks about her real work: what the shop sells, and how many bills a day. So every task in her workbook uses her own shop.
Stuck? The AI teacher explains it a new way, as many times as she needs, but never does it for her. She can also paste a ready prompt into her own ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, for free.
When she uploads her file, the checker reads every cell. It notices she typed one total by hand instead of using a formula.

## 5. A student learns to speak (1:32 – 1:47)

**SCREEN:** Rohit taps Record on a speaking task. The result shows words a minute, filler words, and the feedback.

**VOICE:**
Rohit wants to speak English in interviews without fear.
He records his answer. Meta's voice model turns it into text, and the checker looks at what he said, how fast, and how often he said "um".

## 6. Badge, points, and the next step (1:47 – 2:12)

**SCREEN:** A badge page: "Excel Basics, earned by Priya", with "+92 skill points". The leaderboard, with Priya's row highlighted. Back on the skill page, Advanced now shows as unlocked. Then a "fix-up workbook" opening.

**VOICE:**
Pass, and you get a badge you can share, listing exactly what you can now do, and skill points on the leaderboard.
The badge also opens the next level. Advanced is not just a harder book: the AI reads the Basic work it checked and starts with exactly what was weak. Pro, the last level, takes you to mastery.
Not there yet? You get a short fix-up workbook, free, for just the parts that need more practice.

## 7. How it is built (2:12 – 2:32)

**SCREEN:** A simple diagram: Muse Spark, SAM 3.1, Muse Voice Transcribe, Muse Image, each with what it does.

**VOICE:**
Everything runs on Meta's models.
Muse Spark asks the follow-up questions, writes every workbook, teaches, and marks the work from photos.
SAM measures handwriting. Muse Voice Transcribe hears speaking. Muse Image draws each learner's cover.
And because our learners include children, we never use the mode where Meta can train on their work.

## 8. Why it matters (2:32 – 2:45)

**SCREEN:** The cost table, then the "Get it printed" card on a workbook, then growthgoal.in/learn, then the GrowthGoal logo.

**VOICE:**
A whole Basic level, with the help and the checks, costs us under twenty rupees of AI. That is why we can teach for the price of a book, and even print it and send it home.
It's live today at growthgoal.in slash learn.
Because everyone deserves a patient teacher.
